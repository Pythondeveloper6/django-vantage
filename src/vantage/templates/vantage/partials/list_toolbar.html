{% load vantage i18n %}{% comment %} The list page's control band — one strip, not four. Everything here answers "how am I looking at this list": the search box, the trash tab, the saved view, the grouping, and (when there are rows) the grid's own density and column controls. They used to be four stacked strips between the page title and the first row of data, each one a line of chrome the reader had to pass before reaching what they came for. One band means the table starts higher and the filter rail beside it starts level with the data rather than with an empty gutter. Nothing is removed: the saved-view tabs and the group-by chips became menus, which keeps every destination one click away instead of spending a whole line on each. It renders inside the [data-grid] element on purpose — vantage-grid.js scopes every one of its lookups to that root, so the density and column controls have to live under it. That is the only structural constraint on where this band may sit. {% endcomment %}
