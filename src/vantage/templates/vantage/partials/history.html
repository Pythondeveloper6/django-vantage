{% comment %} The per-record history panel. Rendered only when the viewer may read the audit log — `history` is None otherwise, not an empty list, because "nobody has touched this record" and "you may not see who has" must not look the same. When django-simple-history is installed and covers this model, its timeline renders *alongside* ours rather than instead of it. The two answer different questions: theirs is what the row looked like at each point, ours is who touched it, from where, and through which panel. Neither is a substitute for the other, which is exactly why we ship our own instead of requiring theirs. {% endcomment %} {% if history is not None %}
