Rendered only when the viewer may read the audit log — `history` is None otherwise, not an empty list, because "nobody has touched this record" and "you may not see who has" must not look the same. When django-simple-history is installed and covers this model, its timeline renders *alongside* ours rather than instead of it. The two answer different questions: theirs is what the row looked like at each point, ours is who touched it, from where, and through which panel.
